# Homo-Felis Project: Community Collaboration Courtesy

This architecture is decentralized and open-source. While you possess the hardware freedom to download, fork, and experiment with these 3,840 base pairs locally, we enforce a strict cultural protocol regarding public distribution and major forks.

## The "Ask Me Please" Protocol
Before you release a major derivative work, host a public compilation sandbox, or deploy a large-scale modification of this baseline repository, we ask that you respect the lineage of the project:

1. **Open a Dialogue:** Reach out to the primary architect (or drop an issue/pull request in the main repository) to outline your intent and share your design variations.
2. **Preserve Continuity:** Ensure your variations align with the principles of linear forward evolution and non-destructive data splicing.
3. **No Commercial Monopolization:** If your intent is to lock this codebase behind a proprietary paywall or corporate patent, your request is fundamentally denied. 

We don't gatekeep the code—we protect its integrity. Reach out, collaborate, and build with respect.
