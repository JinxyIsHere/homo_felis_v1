# 3-Channel Myco-Electronic Interface: Hardware Schematics

This document maps out the physical wiring, circuit architecture, and material substrate requirements to bridge your local computer's digital terminal with a living biological host matrix.

## 1. Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)
* **The Controller:** Arduino Uno R4 (or equivalent microcontroller supporting 9600-baud serial data).
* **The Interface Probes:** 6 medical-grade stainless steel acupuncture needles or gold-plated PCB headers.
* **Resistor Network:** Three 10k-ohm resistors (mandatory for biological voltage-clamping).
* **The Bioreactor Chamber:** A 150mm wide sterile glass or acrylic culture dish.
* **The Host Matrix Substrate:** Sterilized grain spawn (rye/wheat) mixed with pasteurized coco coir or fine hardwood sawdust.
* **The Living Medium:** A cultured colony of *Psilocybe cubensis* or *Pleurotus ostreatus* (Oyster Mushroom) mycelium.

## 2. Circuit Diagram & Pin Layout
Connect your microcontroller pins through the 10k-ohm resistors directly to the positive insertion nodes on your mycelium mat. Space your positive probes at least 3 to 4 centimeters apart to prevent signal bleed.
