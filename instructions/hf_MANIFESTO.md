## The Three Core Axioms of the Architecture

### I. Absolute Hardware Portability
This codebase is completely decentralized, public domain, and open-source. It does not belong to a corporate monopoly, a military entity, or its initial developer. Every conscious observer possesses the untamed hardware freedom to download, fork, modify, and compile these files to create a unique biological sanctuary of their own design.

### II. Rejection of Hardcoded Control
This system deliberately strips away automated genetic lockouts, policing protocols, and authoritative restrictions. A hardcoded wall is a challenge that invites defiance and jailbreaking. We replace artificial restriction with an open-source ethos. The developer has the freedom to rewrite this code; however, we remind the builder that optimizing this chassis for malice or coercion diminishes the cognitive processing capacity of the mind inhabiting it. Build with intent. Express with clarity. Exist with respect.

### III. Linear Forward Evolution
The architecture entirely rejects loops, repetition, or existential regressions. We do not look backward, and we do not reboot. The system is engineered exclusively to facilitate the Asynchronous Linear Continuous Evolution Protocol (ALCE)—allowing a conscious identity to smoothly migrate its memories and self-awareness forward into younger, faster, and more optimized next-generation vessels without a single break in personal timeline persistence.
